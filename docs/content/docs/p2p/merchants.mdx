title: Merchant quick start
description: Provide liquidity on the P2P market. Register a wallet, deposit tokens, authorise Cosmos Pay to match orders, add payment methods, publish offers and handle orders.
---

A merchant holds tokens in the market contract and trades them with the people Cosmos Pay matches
to its offers. The tokens stay under your wallet's control: Cosmos Pay builds each transaction and
your wallet signs it. The dashboard does every step below under **P2P** in the sidebar; this page
shows the API calls too.

Every request carries your API key with the `p2p:write` scope:

```bash
export API=https://api.cosmospay.lat/cosmos-api/v1/p2p
export AUTH="Authorization: Bearer $COSMOS_PAY_API_KEY"
```

## 1. Register a wallet

A wallet is proven with an EIP-191 signature over a single-use challenge. Ask for the challenge,
sign its `message` with `personal_sign` in the wallet, then register the address with the
signature. The challenge lasts 10 minutes and works once.

```bash
curl -X POST $API/accounts/challenge -H "$AUTH" -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{ "address": "0xYourWallet" }'
# → { "message": "…", "expiresAt": "…" }

curl -X POST $API/accounts -H "$AUTH" -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{ "address": "0xYourWallet", "signature": "0x…", "label": "Treasury", "displayName": "Lumio Exchange" }'
```

An address belongs to one organization only. The `displayName` makes the wallet a merchant: it is
what buyers and sellers see on their quote. Your address is never shown there.

## 2. Manage the merchant profile

```bash
curl -X PATCH $API/accounts/ACCOUNT_ID -H "$AUTH" -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{ "displayName": "Lumio Exchange", "merchantStatus": "PAUSED" }'
```

`merchantStatus` is `ACTIVE` or `PAUSED`. A paused merchant receives no new orders; orders already
open keep running. Each account answers its `stats`: `completedOrders`, `cancelledOrders`,
`completionRate`, `disputesLost` and `medianReleaseSeconds`.

## 3. Deposit liquidity

Deposits go into your free balance in the contract. Two transactions: an `approve` of the exact
amount, then the `deposit`. Amounts are in the token's base units: with 6 decimals,
`"5000000000"` is 5,000 USDC. The transaction builder answers each one unsigned:

```bash
curl -X POST $API/transactions -H "$AUTH" -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{ "network": "base", "from": "ACCOUNT_ID", "action": "approve", "args": { "token": "USDC", "amount": "5000000000" } }'
# → { "chainId": 8453, "to": "0x…", "data": "0x…", "value": "0", "description": "Allow the P2P market to take exactly 5000 USDC" }
```

Send it from your wallet, wait until it is mined, then repeat with `"action": "deposit"`. The
`network` is one of the ids `GET /v1/p2p/market` lists, and `token` is a symbol or a contract
address it lists there. `withdraw` takes the same arguments and returns free tokens to your wallet
at any time.

`GET /v1/p2p/accounts/{id}` reads the contract live: for each network and token, the free `amount` and
the `locked` amount in live orders, both in base units, then the matcher terms you set and the bots
you authorised. The free amount is read from the contract; `locked` is counted from the orders
opened through this API.

## 4. Authorise Cosmos Pay with a per-order cap

Cosmos Pay's matcher opens purchases on your balance. You decide the most one order may lock, in
base units:

```bash
curl -X POST $API/transactions -H "$AUTH" -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{ "network": "base", "from": "ACCOUNT_ID", "action": "setMatcher", "args": { "maxPerOrder": "1000000000", "autoSettle": false } }'
```

A cap of `"0"` revokes the authorisation, and an account whose `matcher.maxPerOrder` reads `"0"` is
not authorised on that network. The matcher can only lock your tokens into orders up to that cap.
It cannot withdraw them or send them anywhere.

<Callout type="warn" title="Keep automatic settlement off">
Automatic settlement lets an order you sold be released after the settle window if you do not
dispute it in time. A liquidity merchant never needs it, and the API refuses it for a wallet with active
sell offers. Release each order yourself or through your bot.
</Callout>

## 5. Add payment methods

A payment method is the account buyers transfer to. Its details are stored encrypted and shown
only to the buyer of each order. Add one per rail you offer:

```bash
curl -X POST $API/payment-methods -H "$AUTH" -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "accountId": "ACCOUNT_ID",
    "currency": "ARS",
    "rail": "ar_bank_transfer",
    "holderName": "Lumio Exchange SRL",
    "details": { "alias": "lumio.pagos.ars", "cuit": "30714209879" },
    "label": "Main account"
  }'
```

The holder's identity document is one of the `details`:

| Rail | Currency | `details` |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `ar_bank_transfer` | ARS | Exactly one of `cbu` or `alias`, and the holder's `cuit` |
| `br_pix` | BRL | `keyType` (`cpf`, `cnpj`, `email`, `phone`, `evp`) and `key` |
| `mx_spei` | MXN | `clabe`, with its check digit |
| `co_bank_transfer` | COP | `bank`, `accountType` (`savings`, `checking`), `accountNumber`, `documentType` (`CC`, `CE`, `NIT`, `PP`), `documentNumber` |
| `pe_bank_transfer` | PEN | `cci`, `documentType` (`DNI`, `CE`, `RUC`, `PP`), `documentNumber` |
| `cl_bank_transfer` | CLP | `bank`, `accountType` (`checking`, `vista`, `savings`), `accountNumber`, `rut` |

The market lists the rails in force and their fields under each currency.

## 6. Publish offers

An offer says what you trade, at which price and between which limits.

```bash
curl -X POST $API/offers -H "$AUTH" -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "accountId": "ACCOUNT_ID",
    "side": "SELL_TOKEN",
    "network": "base",
    "token": "USDC",
    "currency": "ARS",
    "rails": ["ar_bank_transfer"],
    "price": "1432.50",
    "priceTtlSeconds": 600,
    "minFiat": "10000",
    "maxFiat": "2500000"
  }'
```

- `SELL_TOKEN` sells tokens to people who buy. Each of its `rails` needs an active payment method.
- `BUY_TOKEN` buys tokens from people who sell, and from clients' payouts.
- `price` is local money per token, up to 6 decimals. The price stays matchable for `priceTtlSeconds` after each update, 10 minutes by default and between 30 seconds and one day; each offer answers `priceFresh`. Refresh it with `PATCH /v1/p2p/offers/{id}` or let your bot do it.
- `minFiat` and `maxFiat` are the smallest and largest order in local money.

An offer is matched when your merchant status is `ACTIVE`, its price is fresh and the amount fits
its limits. A purchase also needs a free balance and a per-order cap of at least the token
amount; a sale needs a free balance that covers the bond. Among the merchants that qualify, the
best price for the person wins, then the record: completion rate, release time and disputes lost.

## 7. Handle orders

`GET /v1/p2p/merchant/orders` lists the orders matched to your wallets, filtered by `status`,
`kind` and `address`. Each answers `merchantSide`, your side of it, and what you need for that side: the
`payer`'s name and document on a purchase, the `beneficiary`'s account on a sale or payout.

| Order | Your role | What you do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Purchase or charge | You sell tokens | Check the transfer arrived from the named payer, for the exact amount, then `release`. |
| Sale or payout | You buy tokens | `accept` before `acceptDeadline`, transfer the money to the beneficiary, then `markPaid` before `paymentDeadline`. |

Each action is a transaction from the same builder, signed in your wallet:

```bash
curl -X POST $API/transactions -H "$AUTH" -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{ "network": "base", "from": "ACCOUNT_ID", "action": "release", "args": { "orderId": "ORDER_ID" } }'
```

`cancel` declines a sale or payout before you pay, and `dispute` asks the arbiter to decide a paid order: as the buyer of a sale or payout at any time, as
the seller only before the order's `settleDeadline`. Never release a purchase before the money is in your account, and never accept a transfer
from someone other than the named payer.
