description: Let your users buy and sell stablecoins with local money. Cosmos Pay picks the merchant, the market contract locks the tokens and every party signs with its own wallet.

The **P2P market** lets people buy and sell stablecoins for local money: USDC, and USDT where the
network has it, against a bank transfer, a PIX or a SPEI. The person types an amount and Cosmos Pay
picks the merchant. There is no order book to browse and no counterparty to choose.

The tokens of every order are locked in the market's smart contract until the seller releases
them or a deadline returns them. Nobody can move them anywhere else, Cosmos Pay included.

## Who takes part

| Role | Who | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Client | Your integration | Opens orders for its users: purchases, sales, charges and payouts. |
| Merchant | A liquidity provider | Holds tokens in the contract, publishes offers, receives or sends the local money and releases or accepts orders. |
| Matcher | Cosmos Pay | Picks the merchant for each order and opens purchases and payouts on the merchant's balance, up to the cap the merchant set. |
| Arbiter | Cosmos Pay operations | Resolves disputes from an external multisig wallet. |

One organization can be a client, a merchant or both. Each registers the wallets it trades with,
and each wallet signs its own transactions.

## Networks and tokens

The market runs on the EVM networks where its contract is deployed. `GET /v1/p2p/market` lists
them with each contract address, its tokens and its protocol fee, the currencies and the rails with
their fields, the bond, the time windows and the matcher's address. Read it when your integration
starts instead of hard-coding networks. Each network also answers
`nativeSymbol`, `explorerUrl` and a public `rpcUrl`, what a wallet needs to add it. When it answers `available: false`, the market is not
offered on your key's tier yet.

```bash
curl https://api.cosmospay.lat/cosmos-api/v1/p2p/market \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $COSMOS_PAY_API_KEY"
```

Your API key picks the tier: `dv_` keys trade on test networks, `prod_` keys on mainnets.

## Amounts

Token amounts are integer strings in the token's base units, in requests and in responses: with
6 decimals, `"25000000"` is 25 USDC. Every order, quote and charge also answers `token`, the
contract address, with `tokenSymbol` and `tokenDecimals`. Local money is a decimal string, such as
`"85000.50"`, up to the currency's decimals.

## How an order moves

A **purchase** gives your user tokens for local money:

1. You ask for a quote with the amount in local money: `POST /v1/p2p/quotes`. The quote names the price, the token amount, the fees, the bond, the windows and the merchant's public record. It lasts 60 seconds.
2. You open the order from the quote: `POST /v1/p2p/orders`. The matcher locks the merchant's tokens in the contract, and the order answers the merchant's account in `paymentDetails` and its `paymentDeadline`.
3. Your user transfers the exact amount from an account in their own name before the deadline. You report it with `POST /v1/p2p/orders/{id}/paid`.
4. The merchant sees the money arrive and releases the order. The tokens reach your user's wallet.

A **sale** gives your user local money for tokens:

1. You ask for a quote with the token amount.
2. You open the order. The response holds two unsigned transactions for your user's wallet, `approve` and `openWithDeposit`. The user signs and sends both, and their tokens are locked in the contract.
3. The merchant accepts before the acceptance deadline and locks a bond of 10% of the order.
4. The merchant transfers the local money to your user's account and marks the order paid.
5. Your user releases the tokens. If nobody disputes the order, it can be released by anyone once the settle window has passed. Its `settleDeadline` says when; the seller may dispute only before it.

Charges and payouts are the same two flows, opened by your organization for someone else. See
[Collections](/p2p/collections/) and [Payouts](/p2p/payouts/).

## Statuses

Every order answers one of these statuses. They change only when the contract's events are
indexed, so what you read always matches the chain.

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `pending` | The order is being opened on-chain, or a sale waits for the seller's signature. |
| `awaiting_acceptance` | A sale or payout waits for the merchant to accept and lock its bond. |
| `awaiting_payment` | The tokens are locked. The buyer side transfers the local money. |
| `paid` | The transfer was reported. The seller checks it and releases. |
| `disputed` | One side opened a dispute. The arbiter decides. |
| `completed` | The seller released. The buyer received the tokens. |
| `cancelled` | The order was cancelled before it completed. The tokens went back to the seller. |
| `expired` | A deadline passed. The tokens went back to the seller. |
| `resolved` | The arbiter decided a dispute. |
| `failed` | The order never opened: the transaction reverted or the seller never signed. |

## Time windows

The market answers its windows in seconds under `windows`:

| Window | Default | What it limits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `buyPaymentWindow` | 30 minutes | On a purchase or a charge, the buyer side transfers the money and marks the order paid. |
| `sellAcceptWindow` | 5 minutes | On a sale or a payout, the merchant accepts and locks its bond. |
| `sellPaymentWindow` | 30 minutes | On a sale or a payout, the merchant then transfers the money and marks the order paid. |
| `sellSettleWindow` | 6 hours | Once a sale is paid, it can be released after this window unless someone disputes it. A payout gets it only with automatic settlement on. |

When a deadline passes, the order is cancelled and its tokens return to the seller.

## Fees

Two fees can apply. Both are taken from what the buyer receives and fixed when the order opens.

- The protocol fee, set by Cosmos Pay per network and listed by the market as `protocolFeeBps`. The contract caps it at 3%.
- An integrator fee that a client may add to its own users' orders, up to the market's `maxIntegratorFeeBps`, 5% today. Pass `integratorFeeBps` on the quote; it is paid on-chain to `integratorAccountId`, one of the client's registered wallets.

Every quote shows both fees and the net amount before anyone signs.

## Access

API keys need the `p2p:read` scope to read the market and `p2p:write` to register wallets, publish
offers and open orders, charges and payouts. Create them in the dashboard: [Get your API key](/api-keys/).
The dashboard covers the whole market under **P2P** in the sidebar. Every endpoint, with its
schemas, is in the [P2P market API reference](/api/p2p-market/).

Cosmos Pay's own P2P app on the payments domain runs on this same API, with the same rules.
